# Event Tree Analysis (Model M1): Summary Tables

Generated by `src/eta_pipeline.py` from the PHMSA gas distribution incident file.

## 1. Scope

- The tree classifies recorded outcomes among reportable incidents (49 CFR Part 191). Branch shares are conditional on an incident having been reported; they are not failure rates per mile-year.
- In 72 Other Outside Force Damage records a nearby fire or explosion damaged the pipe, so ignition came first. The tree does not claim a fixed time order for every incident.
- Minor leaks below the reporting thresholds are not in the data, which raises the apparent ignition share.
